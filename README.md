# Manual Operated Ropeway – Kamand Connect

A 10 m functional manual ropeway prototype designed and fabricated as a low-cost, portable transportation system for mountainous and sloped terrain.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Manual Operated Ropeway – Kamand Connect** is a manually powered ropeway system developed to transport people and goods across steep and difficult terrains where conventional road-based transportation can be challenging.

The project follows a complete **CAD-to-Hardware workflow**. The complete mechanical system was first designed and assembled in **SolidWorks**, followed by structural analysis in **ANSYS Workbench**. Based on the finalized design, the components were fabricated and assembled into a functional **10 m physical prototype**.

The system integrates a steel structural frame, wire-rope mechanism, pulleys, shafts, bearings, gears, cabin and a pedal-driven mechanism.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Develop a **low-cost and portable ropeway system** for mountainous terrain.
- Create a complete **3D CAD model and mechanical assembly** in SolidWorks.
- Analyze the structural performance using **ANSYS Workbench**.
- Fabricate and assemble the complete physical hardware.
- Experimentally validate the fabricated prototype.
- Evaluate structural safety, deformation and scalability of the design.

## 💻 CAD Design – SolidWorks

The complete ropeway system was designed and assembled in **SolidWorks** before physical fabrication.

The CAD assembly included:

* Structural support trestles
* Base and top frame
* Diagonal reinforcement members
* Cabin structure
* Seating arrangement
* Pulley assembly
* Shaft and bearing arrangement
* Gear mechanism
* Pedal-drive mechanism
* Mounting brackets
* Connector plates

The finalized CAD design was then used as the reference for fabrication and physical assembly.

---

## 🔬 Structural Analysis – ANSYS Workbench

A **static structural analysis** was performed using ANSYS Workbench to evaluate the mechanical response of the ropeway structure under critical loading conditions.

### Analysis Parameters

The analysis considered:

* Structural stress
* Equivalent stress distribution
* Total deformation
* Cable loading
* Critical structural regions
* Factor of Safety
* Load-bearing performance

Special attention was given to stress-sensitive regions such as:

* Bolt connections
* Bracket interfaces
* Pulley supports
* Structural joints

Mesh refinement was applied in critical regions to improve the accuracy of the simulation.

---

## 🔩 Fabrication & Hardware Implementation

Unlike a simulation-only project, the complete CAD design was converted into a **physical 10 m functional hardware prototype**.

The fabrication and assembly process included:

1. Manufacturing the structural members.
2. Fabricating the support and reinforcement structure.
3. Assembling the steel frame.
4. Installing pulleys and shafts.
5. Integrating bearings and gears.
6. Installing the wire-rope system.
7. Fabricating and assembling the cabin.
8. Integrating the pedal-drive mechanism.
9. Performing final mechanical assembly.
10. Testing the complete prototype.

---

## 🧪 Experimental Validation

The fabricated prototype was experimentally tested to compare real-world performance with the ANSYS simulation.

### Measurements

**Cable Tension**

Cable tension was measured using a handheld tensiometer.

**Vertical Sag**

Vertical sag was measured using a laser-level reference system.

The experimental measurements were compared with simulation results to evaluate the structural response and performance of the fabricated prototype.

---

## 📊 Analysis Results

The project included structural and mechanical evaluation of the prototype.

| Parameter                     | Prototype / Analysis  |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Prototype Span                | 10 m                  |
| Maximum Prototype Stress      | 471 MPa               |
| Prototype Factor of Safety    | ≈ 1.27                |
| Prototype Vertical Deflection | 0.017037 m            |
| Projected Cable Tension       | ~2–3 kN               |
| Pedal Power                   | 150–200 W             |
| Prototype Cost                | Approximately ₹46,000 |

> **Note:** Full-scale analysis indicated higher structural stresses than the selected steel's yield strength. Therefore, further structural reinforcement, improved load distribution or higher-strength materials would be required before full-scale implementation.
